Privacy policy

Last updated 24 October 2026

We collect as little as we can.

  • This website has no cookies, no analytics and no tracking.
  • PressBridge runs on your server. Your site files are never sent to us.
  • When you buy, we get your name, email, country and order details from our reseller. We never see your card.

Who is responsible for your data

Paul Negrea, an individual based in Romania, is the data controller for the personal data described here. Contact: pressbridge.support@gmail.com.

We don't sell your data, use it for advertising, or send marketing emails. We may email license holders about important updates, security fixes or changes to these policies.

The PressBridge software

PressBridge runs on servers you control. It doesn't send your site files, configuration or usage data to us. The data it handles goes only where you point it:

  • to the AI assistant you connect it to, such as Claude, under that provider's privacy terms;
  • if you use Watch, to the alert channels you configure (email, Slack, Discord, ntfy or a webhook) and, if you add your own API key, to Anthropic.

For the data on your clients' sites, you remain the controller (or processor for your clients); we don't process it.

Service providers

  • Our reseller (merchant of record). Processes your payment and is an independent controller for the payment data it collects, under its own privacy policy, linked from the checkout page.
  • GitHub. Hosts this website and the free edition. GitHub may log your IP address for security.
  • Google Fonts. Serves the fonts on this website, so Google receives your IP address when the page loads.
  • Gmail (Google). Hosts our support mailbox.

Some of these providers are based in the United States. Transfers rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

Your rights

If you are in the EU or the UK, you can ask us for a copy of your data, and ask us to correct, delete or export it, or to stop or restrict using it. Write to pressbridge.support@gmail.com and we'll answer within one month. We may need to keep order records where the law requires it.

You can also complain to a data protection authority. In Romania, that is the ANSPDCP (dataprotection.ro); you can also go to the authority in your own country.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change matters for license holders, we'll email them.
